## Customer Health Monitoring

### Health Indicators
- **Usage**: Invoice volume, payment volume
- **Performance**: Response times, error rates
- **Satisfaction**: Support tickets, feedback
- **Engagement**: Feature adoption, training completion

### Health Scoring
```
Score = (Usage * 0.3) + (Performance * 0.3) + (Satisfaction * 0.2) + (Engagement * 0.2)

Green (80-100): Healthy customer
Yellow (60-79): At-risk customer
Red (<60): Critical customer
```

### Intervention Triggers
- **Score drops below 70**: Schedule check-in call
- **Error rate exceeds 1%**: Investigate and resolve
- **Support tickets increase**: Assess root cause
- **Feature adoption low**: Provide additional training
